#AURORE- A Fashion Website
AURORE is a fashion brand website for affordable and comfortable clothes that tell your story.
Our Mission: To create affordable and comfortable clothes that tell your story.
Our Vision:To become Nigeria's most loved youth fashion brand for style, quality and confidence.

##Features
-Home page with brand story
-Shop / collection page with product images
-Product details and prices
-Add to cart
-About Us - Mission and Vision
-Contact section

##How To Open The Website
-Download/clone this repo
-Open the folder in VS Code
Double-click 'index.html' OR right-click 'index.html'> Open with Live server.

##Files In This Project
-'index.html' - Main website page
-'style.css' -Design and styling
-'script.js' -Cart and buttons functionality
-'logo.jpg' and product images - Photos for products
